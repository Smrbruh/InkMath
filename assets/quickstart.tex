\subsection*{Start with a thought}
Probability is a language for reasoning about uncertainty.
If $P(B)>0$, conditional probability is
\[
P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}.
\]
Type inline mathematics between dollar signs, such as $E[X]=\mu$.
Put a larger equation between \verb|\[| and \verb|\]|.

\subsection*{Give an argument room to breathe}
For independent observations with variance $\sigma^2$,
\begin{align*}
\operatorname{Var}(\bar X)
&=\operatorname{Var}\left(\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nX_i\right)\\
&=\frac1{n^2}\sum_{i=1}^n\operatorname{Var}(X_i)
=\frac{\sigma^2}{n}.
\end{align*}
This is why averaging reduces uncertainty.
